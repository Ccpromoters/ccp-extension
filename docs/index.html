CCP Extension

A free Chrome extension for job seekers and the career-support teams who work for them: opens your job boards, keeps a truthful record of every application, sorts recruiter mail, and tailors your resume to any job description – all inside your browser.

What it does

Open job boards

LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Dice, Monster, Gmail and Chrome history in one click, or every time Chrome starts.

Applications tracker

Reads your own browser history, Gmail confirmations and the boards' "Applied" pages. One list: applied, likely, viewed – with CSV export and a printable weekly report.

Recruiter inbox

Outreach, right-to-represent requests, interview invitations, rejections and confirmations, tagged by field.

Resume tailoring

Upload your final resume once. Per job, the extension adds the JD's missing skills to your skills rows and changes at most a couple of bullets – same layout, every other line untouched.

Honest gap report

What the job asks for that you already have, what you clearly lack, what to confirm – and an experience-years warning. Dates and years are never changed.

Skills gap vs. the market

Postings you open are collected locally so you can see which skills your target jobs keep asking for.

Install in one minute

  1. Download ccp-extension-vX.Y.Z.zip from Releases and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Pin the CCP icon. The settings page opens: add the candidate's name, the master resume (.docx) and an AI key for tailoring.

Full guide: docs/install.md

Privacy

No server, no account, no analytics. Everything stays in your Chrome profile. The only network request is the AI call you trigger yourself, sent directly to the provider you configured. Read the privacy policy.
